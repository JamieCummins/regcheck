Step 1

Compare registrations with papers

RegCheck extracts and compares text from your registration and paper, so you can see if and how a study deviated from its plan.

Beta RegCheck is currently undergoing evaluation by our research team. For now, use with caution.

Choose a parser

This determines how RegCheck reads your paper. PyMuPDF (recommended) runs entirely on our server and keeps all selectable text, including author notes and footnotes. GROBID is tuned for typeset journal PDFs but can fail on non-standard layouts. DPT-2 handles less standard academic documents.

Choose a model

ChatGPT and Claude Opus 4.8 are generally most accurate, but can be slower. GPT-OSS 120B is OpenAI's open-weight model (served via Groq). Llama 4 Maverick (via Groq) is typically fastest but less accurate. DeepSeek can perform well but may be slower.

Lower effort is faster and cheaper; higher is more thorough but slower. Applies to ChatGPT only.

Comparison dimensions

RegCheck compares the registration and paper on each of these dimensions in the order you specify.

Add at least one dimension to continue — RegCheck needs at least one thing to compare.

Append previous outputs?

Should RegCheck include earlier dimension responses when prompting for later dimensions? “Yes” can improve performance but introduces dependence between decisions; “No” keeps outputs independent.

If yes, we fetch the registration directly from ClinicalTrials.gov. Otherwise, you upload a preregistration file.
