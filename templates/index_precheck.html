Check your study plan before the study runs.

For researchers writing or reviewing preregistrations. PreCheck evaluates how completely and unambiguously a registration specifies the planned study — so gaps get fixed before data collection, not discovered after.

    PreCheck assesses each quality criterion: is the plan specified precisely enough that a deviation could later be detected?
