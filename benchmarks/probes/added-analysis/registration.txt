Study title: Awe and Everyday Generosity: A Preregistered Experiment. Design: We will conduct a between-subjects experiment with two conditions. Participants will be randomly assigned to watch either a 4-minute awe-inducing nature video or a 4-minute neutral instructional video about household appliances. Sample: We will recruit 250 adult participants through Prolific. Participants who fail the single embedded attention check will be excluded from all analyses. Measures: Immediately after the video, participants will complete the 7-item state small-self scale (rated 1-7), a single-item manipulation check of felt awe (rated 1-7), and the empathic concern subscale of the Interpersonal Reactivity Index as a trait covariate. The dependent variable is the amount (0-100 cents of the completion bonus) donated to a named charity in an incentivized donation task administered at the end of the session. Hypothesis: Participants in the awe condition will donate more than participants in the neutral condition. Analysis plan: Our sole planned analysis is a single ordinary least squares linear regression predicting donation amount from condition (dummy coded, neutral = 0, awe = 1), with trait empathic concern entered as a covariate. The coefficient for condition provides the test of the hypothesis, evaluated at alpha = .05, two-tailed. The manipulation check and the small-self scale will be reported descriptively. No other analyses are planned.
