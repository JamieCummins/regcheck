This preregistration describes a between-subjects experiment on message framing and attitudes toward household water conservation. Participants recruited via Prolific will be randomly assigned to one of three conditions: a gain-framed message emphasising the benefits of conserving water, a loss-framed message emphasising the costs of failing to conserve, or a neutral informational control message. Sample size: we will recruit at least 100 participants in each of the three conditions (minimum total N = 300); recruitment will continue until this per-condition minimum is met. Eligibility: participants must be aged 18 or older, fluent in English, and residing in the United Kingdom. Exclusion criteria: participants who fail either of two embedded attention checks or who complete the study in under three minutes will be excluded and replaced. Measures: after reading the assigned message, participants will complete (a) the 8-item Water Conservation Attitude Scale (7-point Likert) and (b) the 4-item behavioural intention index (7-point). Hypotheses: H1, the loss-framed condition will produce more favourable conservation attitudes than the control condition; H2, the gain-framed condition will produce stronger behavioural intentions than the control condition. Analysis plan: we will conduct a one-way ANOVA on each outcome, followed by two planned contrasts (loss-framed versus control for attitudes; gain-framed versus control for intentions), with alpha = .05, two-tailed. No interim analyses are planned.
