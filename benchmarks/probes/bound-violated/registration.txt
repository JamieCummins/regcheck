Preregistration: Construal level and pro-environmental intentions. This study tests whether inducing an abstract (versus concrete) construal mindset increases pro-environmental behavioural intentions. Confirmatory hypothesis: participants primed with an abstract construal mindset will report higher pro-environmental intentions than participants primed with a concrete mindset. Design: single-factor, two-cell between-subjects experiment; participants will be randomly assigned to the abstract or concrete condition using the Qualtrics randomiser. Manipulation: the Why/How priming task (Freitas et al., 2004); participants in the abstract condition generate "why" explanations for maintaining good health, whereas participants in the concrete condition generate "how" descriptions of the same activity. Sample: we will recruit at least 300 participants from the university undergraduate subject pool. An a priori power analysis indicated that 300 participants provide 90% power to detect an effect of d = 0.38 at alpha = .05 (two-tailed). Exclusion criteria: participants who fail the single instructed attention-check item ("please select 'strongly disagree'") will be excluded prior to analysis. Measures: the primary outcome is the 12-item Pro-Environmental Behavioural Intentions scale (1 = very unlikely, 7 = very likely), averaged into a single composite score; the manipulation check is the 25-item Behaviour Identification Form (BIF). Analysis plan: condition means on the intentions composite will be compared using an independent-samples Welch's t-test, alpha = .05, two-tailed; as a manipulation check, condition means on the BIF will be compared using the same independent-samples Welch's t-test, alpha = .05, two-tailed. No additional analyses are planned.
